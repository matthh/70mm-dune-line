## Maintainability

### L-1 · `.at-row.clickable` hover/cursor styles have no effect — open

**File:** `app/globals.css:504, 662–664`

`display: contents` removes the box model; `background`, `cursor`, padding,
and border-radius on `.at-row.clickable` do not render. Click events still
fire correctly. Open since 2026-06-09.

**Recommended fix:** Change `.at-row` to `display: grid; grid-column: 1 / -1`
or restructure leaderboard rows so the clickable wrapper is not
`display: contents`.

---

### L-2 · Non-null assertion `m.sum!` in `toCatalogMovie` — open

**File:** `lib/data.ts:425`

Call sites pre-filter `m.sum != null`, so no runtime NaN today. A future
refactor could introduce a silent NaN.

**Recommended fix:** Type `CatalogMovie.sum` as `number | null` and remove
the assertion, or add a runtime guard inside `toCatalogMovie`.

---

### L-3 · Typo key `'Holy/Regligion/Spiritual Month'` in SHORT_NAMES — open

**File:** `lib/data.ts:158`

The typo mirrors the wiki source verbatim. If the wiki corrects the spelling
the override silently stops firing.

**Recommended fix:** Add an inline comment noting the typo is from wiki source
so a future editor does not "fix" it.

---

### L-5 · No unit tests — open

**File:** `lib/data.ts`

`buildTimeline()`, `shortenThemeName()`, `categoryFor()`, and `isRegular()` are
pure functions with no test coverage.

**Recommended fix:** Add `vitest` and a `lib/data.test.ts` covering at minimum
`categoryFor()`, `isRegular()`, `shortenThemeName()`, and the null-episode sort
tiebreaker in `buildTimeline()`.
